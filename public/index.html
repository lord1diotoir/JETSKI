    Sources & caveats
    • FWC Boating & Waterways GIS: layer 9 (Manatee Protection Zones, 68C-22), layer 10 (Boating Restricted Areas, 68D-24) and layer 4 (boat ramp inventory). Fetched 2026-11-16. The 68D layer was last edited in GIS 2025-06-03.
    • 68D-24.006 was repealed in 2013 and is not used.
    • Lauderdale Beach ICW Zone, 68D-24.008(1)(a)15. (effective Feb 3, 2026): rule text checked against the FAC. It is missing from GIS, so the polygon is DIGITIZED. It fills the ICW between the Oakland Park idle zone (1,500 ft south of that bridge) and the Sunrise idle zone (2,250 ft north of that bridge), as the rule defines it. Not survey grade.
    • Hallandale Beach Blvd, 68D-24.008(1)(a)14.c: confirmed in force (adopted in the 9-27-21 amendment). It is Slow Speed Minimum Wake from 400 ft south of the bridge to the Dade/Broward line. The GIS note "To be voted on by Commission 8/5/2021" is out of date.
    • USCG 33 CFR 165.760 (Port Everglades security zones), 165.765 (Port Everglades slow-speed RNA) and 165.711 (aircraft carrier zones) are DIGITIZED from eCFR coordinates and descriptions. Moving zones cannot be mapped. This app has no AIS feed.
    • County and city ordinances are NOT digitized. Municode could not be reached from the build environment, and no polygons were made up. These include Broward County, Fort Lauderdale Ch. 8 §8-166, Lighthouse Point Ch. 90, Deerfield Beach Ch. 74 §74-46 and Dania Beach. Rules in Hollywood, Hallandale Beach and Pompano Beach were not checked.
      • Broward County Code of Ordinances

      • Lighthouse Point Ch. 90 Waterways
      • Deerfield Beach §74-46
      • Dania Beach Code of Ordinances
    • FWC official maps: Broward manatee zone maps (PDF) · Broward BRA maps (PDF)
    • The Broward GeoHub boating-facilities layer is a 2018 PDF, and EEPD_Marine_Facility returned 403. Neither is used. Ramp details come from the FWC inventory plus official pages where noted. No official live ramp-capacity feed exists.
    • Context only, no polygon: Palm Beach County 68D-24.017, Camino Real Bridge to the county line, is Slow Speed on weekends and holidays from 10 am to 6 pm.
    • Miami-Dade: only the FWC manatee layer (68C-22.025) is included. The Miami-Dade base app was not available.
    • Holidays follow § 110.117, F.S. Both the actual and the observed date count as holidays. The Sunrise Blvd Bridge South weekend zone, 68D-24.008(1)(a)7.b., defines holidays by Rule 68D-24.002, F.A.C., which was not checked. The § 110.117 list is used as a cautious stand-in.
    Posted signs prevail. The FAC prevails over GIS (68C-22.010(5)). Not for navigation.
